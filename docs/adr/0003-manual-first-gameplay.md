# Manual-first gameplay

The first version is a manual tabletop: players can move cards freely between zones, draw and shuffle from their own Library, and tap or untap permanents on the Battlefield. Zone scope, visibility, and ordering follow their normal defaults, while public cards can be manipulated by any participant and private Hands and Libraries remain restricted to their owner. The game does not enforce move legality, deck legality, or card effects. This makes friend games playable before building the much broader rules and card-ability system, which can be added in later phases.
