# 07: Nearby Life and Turn Controls

**What to build:** Life Totals and Turn State are readable and editable near the Match Players and cards they describe, so routine updates no longer require a full-width form above the Battlefield.

**Blocked by:** 02: Shared Two-Player Areas.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Each Match Player's Life Total and status are visible near their Player Area, with the existing manual edit behavior preserved.
- [ ] Active Match Player, turn number, phase, and step are visible on the table; participants can still advance, go back, and directly correct Turn State.
- [ ] Updates synchronize across participants and remain informational rather than enforcing rules or restricting actions.
- [ ] Controls remain readable at the initial fitted laptop viewport without adding page scrolling.
- [ ] Browser-level tests cover life and turn changes from one participant appearing correctly for another.
